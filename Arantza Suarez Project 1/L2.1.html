Lila exhales slowly and places the phone back on the couch. His explanation was rushed, but she caught him off guard. She decides that trust isnt proven by certainty, but by their willingness to believe. Besides, Lila wants to believe Alex. He has always been honest before. Why wouldnt he be now?


The next week, Alex takes Lila to a beautiful park after lunch, and surprises her with a proposal.


Overwhelmed with joy, she sees a girl holding a camera walking over.


"Hi! Im Maya, nice to meet you! Im one of Alex's coworkers, and he asked me to help him out with engagement photos since I used to be a photographer. They came out absolutely beautiful! Im so happy for you guys."


Lila feels both relief and a quiet guilt for doubting Alex.
